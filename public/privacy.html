Privacy notice

Privacy information. This notice describes how we handle enquiries and orders.

Data controller and privacy contact

AI Admin Accelerator is the trading name used by the data controller for this business. For privacy enquiries and data rights requests, contact aiadminaccelerator@agentmail.to. For information about purchases and cancellation rights, see our Terms of Service.

Information processed

If you choose to enquire by email, we process the sender address, name and any business or workflow details you voluntarily include in the message, solely to answer your enquiry. We do not operate an unsolicited email marketing list. Stripe processes checkout and payment details. The website processes order status and a restricted-access checkout reference. A transactional email processor delivers purchase confirmations. If you purchase a personalised review, the business name, sector, workflow, tools and goals submitted in the intake are sent to the operator to fulfil the service.

Customer comments and ratings

If you use the feedback form, we receive the display name, email address, optional order reference, rating and written comments you provide. These details are delivered privately to our business support inbox through AgentMail and used to review your feedback, respond where necessary, verify claimed purchases where appropriate and moderate contributions for spam, personal information or abuse. We do not currently publish submitted feedback automatically. If you explicitly agree to publication, we may publish your display name, rating and comment after review; your email address and order reference will not be published. Critical comments are assessed using the same criteria as positive comments.

Processing and moderation of requested feedback is based on our legitimate interests in improving services and maintaining a reliable review area; we seek separate permission before publicly displaying a comment under the form's publication consent. You can withdraw publication permission or ask us to remove a published comment by contacting our privacy inbox. We do not share submitted reviews with Trustpilot automatically and do not claim a Trustpilot rating without a verified profile.

Purpose and lawful bases

Responding to requested enquiries may be necessary to take steps before a contract or for legitimate interests in business communication. We do not use enquiries for unsolicited bulk marketing. Order fulfilment and communications are required for the contract. Limited technical/security records may be processed to secure the service. Appropriate bookkeeping records may be held to satisfy legal obligations. The website does not operate an email marketing list by default.

Providers and transfers

Payment provider: Stripe, where customer card details are entered and processed. Hosting: Render. Business enquiry, support and transactional order delivery: AgentMail. Paid digital toolkit downloads may be sent as email attachments. International data transfers and associated safeguards must be verified by the operator.

Retention and rights

Enquiries arrive in the business mailbox and can be erased upon request where lawful and appropriate. Other information is kept only for defined business, security and legal requirements; actual retention periods must be confirmed. UK data protection law may give you access, correction, deletion, restriction and other rights. You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and contact

This enquiry website has no advertising trackers. Stripe Checkout may set its own cookies. For privacy requests: aiadminaccelerator@agentmail.to.
