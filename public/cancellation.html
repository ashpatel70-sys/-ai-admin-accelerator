Your cancellation rights

UK customers purchasing online generally have a 14-day cancellation period for many distance contracts, subject to statutory exceptions. This includes services, where consumers may be liable for a proportionate charge for work carried out at their express request during the cancellation period.

For digital content supplied without a physical medium, the statutory right to cancel may be lost after downloading or streaming begins only where you expressly consented to immediate supply, acknowledged the loss of that right, and the required contract confirmation was provided. This does not affect your rights if the content is faulty or misdescribed.
