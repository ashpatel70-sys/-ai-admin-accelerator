Frequently asked questions

Everything clear, from the start.

What can I buy immediately?

The £29 Toolkit, £149 Workflow Review, £299 Growth Bundle, £599 Team Productivity Sprint and £999 Operations Transformation Planning are available for one-time card payments. Unpriced digital-product concepts are still in development and cannot be purchased.

How is my purchase delivered?

After verified payment, the toolkit is emailed as a ZIP. All personalised services receive an email requesting your brief. For the £149 written review, delivery is normally within five UK working days after complete intake. Higher-tier consultation or training dates are agreed after your brief is received.

How can I pay?

All five current offers accept secure Stripe card checkout when the service is available. Prices are in GBP with no recurring subscription.

Do I need an AI software subscription?

No subscription is included or required to purchase a toolkit. You may choose to use its resources with third-party tools that have their own subscriptions, terms and usage rules.

Is the business open for visits in London?

No public walk-in premises are advertised. The map indicates the general London service area, not a trading address. Our offers and enquiries are handled remotely.

How do refunds and cancellations work?

See the terms and cancellation information. UK consumer rights and applicable exceptions remain in force. Contact our support inbox for help.
