When is a workflow review useful?

It can be helpful when your team repeats the same administrative task, spends too long drafting standard documents, loses track of enquiries or relies on inconsistent handoffs. A written review helps clarify which steps could be simplified, standardised or supported by AI, before you buy additional tools.
